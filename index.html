About This Initiative

Solving the Market Access Bottleneck in Karenga

The Challenge in Karenga District

Located in the northern tip of Karamoja bordering South Sudan and Kitgum, Karenga has fertile agricultural belts (such as Kapedo, Lokori, and Lobalangit) producing rich yields of sorghum, finger millet, simsim, groundnuts, and famous organic bush honey.

However, farmers routinely face severe challenges:

  • Lack of direct buyer contacts, making farmers dependent on itinerant middlemen who buy at exploitative prices.
  • Post-harvest losses caused by prolonged storage while searching for buyers.
  • Difficulty for external bulk buyers (lodges in Kidepo, schools, grain aggregators in Kotido or Kampala) to locate where surplus produce is stored.

How Karenga AgriMarket Solves It

This platform serves as a lightweight, zero-barrier virtual market square that works on any basic smartphone, tablet, or computer:

  • Zero Intermediary Fees: Buyers dial farmers directly on MTN / Airtel or click to chat on WhatsApp.
  • Offline Persistence: Listings are saved locally on the device and can be shared over SMS or WhatsApp groups.
  • Sub-county Geotargeting: Buyers know exactly which village or trading center in Karenga to collect the produce.
  • Fair Price Transparency: The weekly price bulletin helps farmers know their true crop value.

Role of Community Extension Workers & Parish Chiefs

For farmers without smartphones or digital literacy, village agricultural extension officers, parish chiefs, youth champions, and cooperative leaders can act as digital aggregators—listing produce on their behalf and receiving calls from bulk purchasers.
