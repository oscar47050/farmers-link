# 🌾 Karenga AgriMarket (Karamoja Sub-Region, Uganda)

An easy, direct online marketplace and selling platform created for farmers in **Karenga District, Karamoja Sub-Region** to link directly with potential buyers, eliminating exploitative middlemen and expanding market access.

## 📍 Tailored Specifically for Karenga District

- **Sub-counties Covered:**
  - Karenga Town Council
  - Kapedo Sub-county
  - Lobalangit Sub-county
  - Lokori Sub-county
  - Kawalakol Sub-county
  - Sangar Sub-county

- **Key Produce Categories:**
  - **🌾 Cereals & Grains:** Red & White Sorghum (Serena), Finger Millet (Kalo), Dry Maize grain.
  - **🥜 Legumes & Oilseeds:** White Simsim (Sesame), Groundnuts (G-nuts), Pigeon Peas, Red Beans, Cowpeas.
  - **🍯 Karamoja Bush Honey:** Pure raw wild honey harvested near Kidepo Valley.
  - **🐐 Livestock & Poultry:** Karamoja meat goats, sheep, Zebu cattle, local poultry.
  - **🥬 Tubers & Fresh Garden Produce:** Red onions, tomatoes, cassava, sweet potatoes.

## 🚀 Key Features

1. **Direct Contact Without Middlemen (Zero Fees):**
   - **Call Farmer:** One-tap calling to MTN or Airtel numbers.
   - **WhatsApp Chat:** Automatically formats a ready-to-send WhatsApp order message with the produce name, quantity, price, and location.
   - **SMS Inquiries:** Pre-fills an SMS text for areas with slow internet.

2. **"Sell My Produce" (Farmer Listing Form):**
   - Any farmer, cooperative group, or agricultural extension worker can list their produce in under 1 minute.
   - Saves automatically in your browser storage (`localStorage`) so it works even offline!

3. **"Buyer Demand Board":**
   - Bulk buyers (lodges in Kidepo, schools, grain aggregators from Kotido/Moroto/Kitgum/Juba) can post what they want to purchase with their target budget in Ugandan Shillings (UGX).
   - Local farmers can browse buyer orders and call them directly to deliver.

4. **Karenga Prevailing Price Index (UGX):**
   - Displays benchmark farmgate and town center prices for sorghum, simsim, millet, honey, and goats.
   - Protects rural farmers from being tricked into selling at unfair prices during harvest seasons.

5. **Download Weekly Report (CSV / Excel):**
   - Click **"Download Listings Report"** to export all active produce in Karenga into an Excel-compatible spreadsheet—ideal for District Commercial Officers, NGOs, and Parish Development Model (PDM) extension staff.
